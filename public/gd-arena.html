A room that keeps its own order

The hard part of a voice discussion is not the words. It is knowing when to speak, when to stop and who has the floor.

One voice at a time

A floor controller decides who may speak. The AI only decides what to say, so participants wait for a pause and never talk over each other.

Cut in whenever you like

Start talking and every AI stops mid-sentence. The transcript keeps only the words you actually heard.

Feedback you can check

Each comment links to a moment in the transcript, and each quote is matched against that moment before it appears.

Keeps going when things break

Microphone blocked, connection dropped, an AI call failed: the room adapts and the session carries on.
